# Fonde Senior Basketball

**Rules of the Game** · *Draft 9 · 2026-09-30*

## The rules

1. **First player to the gym finds the clipboard and makes the list.**
2. **Only sign your own name.** You are not allowed to sign for anyone else.
3. **Saturday morning games are timed at 12 minutes.**
4. **A tie is a tie. No overtime.**
5. **Two minutes between games.**
6. **First game: the winner stays up. The loser sits and can re-sign the list.**
7. **Every game after that: play two and sit.**
8. **Both courts are used when 17 or more players are on the list at 10:00 AM.**
9. **Both courts play to the same clock.**
10. **A game that reaches 21 before the clock runs out ends there, and those players rest early.** The next games still start at the same time on both courts.
11. **Saturday morning games start no later than 10:10 AM.**
12. **Players on the list must be ready to play when their name is called.**
13. **After you play two and sit, you re-sign the list yourself.** Nobody signs it for you.
14. **Courtesy: the losing team signs back in the same order they played.** No fighting over who signs first.
